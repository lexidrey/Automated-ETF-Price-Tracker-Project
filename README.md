Project Purpose:
The Automated ETF Price Tracker tracks significant price changes for the VOO ETF. The goal is to notify the email recipients of days on which it will be smart to buy shares of VOO. This will eliminate the need to check the price of VOO manually on a daily basis. It will run every day at 6:00 PM. It will send out an email when either one or both of the following conditions are met:
The closing price of VOO for the day is below the lowest closing price of VOO of the past 3 months. We will consider this a significant price drop and a good time to buy VOO.
The price of VOO has dropped by more than or exactly 4% between today and the date with the highest closing price in the past 10 days. We will consider this a significant price drop and a good time to buy VOO.
